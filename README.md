## About

The **Anturi Quark** is a portable device for detecting minerals in water, in minutes and without a lab. This app is its control panel: it finds the Quark over Bluetooth, sends the measurement parameters, shows the results and exports them to Excel.

## Installation

1. Download **AnturiQuark app.zip** on your phone (or on a computer and copy it to the phone) and extract it.
2. Open `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`.
3. If Android asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for the app you opened it with (Files, Chrome, WhatsApp…).
4. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap **Install anyway**. The app is not distributed through Google Play yet.
5. Open **Anturi Quark App** and accept the Bluetooth permissions.

## Features

- **Techniques:** DC, cyclic voltammetry (CV), differential pulse voltammetry (DPV) and square wave voltammetry (SWV).
- **Current ranges:** ±145 µA, ±1.45 mA and ±14.5 mA, plus auto range for CV.
- **Results:** calibrated and raw current, plotted by sample or time, and the I/V curve.
- **Excel export:** every test is saved as an `.xlsx` file and can be shared from the app.
- **Setups:** save and reload the parameters of each technique.
- **Calibration:** gain and offset correction for each range, using the Quark's internal precision resistor.
- **Test mode:** measures the internal precision resistor instead of the electrode, to check the device.
- **Device status:** battery and temperature, low-battery alert and remote power off.

## Requirements

- Android 7.0 or later, with Bluetooth Low Energy, on a 64-bit phone (practically every phone from 2017 onwards).
- An Anturi Quark device.
